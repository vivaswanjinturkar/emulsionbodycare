Founder's Note

Hi, I’m Dr. Sameera Shiurkar.

As an MBBS doctor, Skin Specialist, and Aesthetic Physician, I’ve spent years understanding the science behind healthy skin. Through that journey, I realised that body care deserves the same thoughtful attention we give to facial skincare.

That belief inspired Emulsion—a collection of handcrafted essentials where every ingredient has a purpose, every formulation is intentional, and every product is made with care.

Thank you for being here. I hope Emulsion becomes a small but meaningful part of your everyday ritual.
