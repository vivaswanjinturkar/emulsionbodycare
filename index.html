Meet the Founder

Beautiful skin begins
with understanding it.

I've always believed that healthy skin is more than what we see on the surface. It reflects balance, care, and an understanding of the remarkable science behind it. I'm Dr. Sameera Shiurkar, a Medical Doctor and Aesthetic Physician, and the founder of Emulsion Bodycare.

My journey into skincare began through medicine. Years of studying the human body and working in aesthetic medicine gave me a deep appreciation for the skin — its complexity, its resilience, and the profound impact that thoughtful care can have on both skin health and self-confidence.

As I worked with patients, I found myself searching for skincare that combined scientific understanding with elegant simplicity — products that felt luxurious yet approachable, where every ingredient served a purpose and every formulation reflected intention rather than excess. That vision became Emulsion.

"Every ingredient is carefully curated for its purpose, every formula crafted with precision and compassion — inspired by the harmony between nature and science."

Building Emulsion has been one of the most meaningful journeys of my life. It is a reflection of everything I value as a doctor and as a founder: curiosity, craftsmanship, authenticity, and genuine care for the people who choose our products. Every jar and bottle represents our commitment to skincare that is beautiful in both experience and purpose.

Thank you for placing your trust in Emulsion. It is our privilege to be part of your skincare journey, and we hope every product reminds you that caring for your skin is one of the simplest, most meaningful forms of self-care.

With gratitude, Dr. Sameera Shiurkar Founder, Emulsion Bodycare — Medical Doctor & Aesthetic Physician
